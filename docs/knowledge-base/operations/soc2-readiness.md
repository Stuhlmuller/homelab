# SOC 2 readiness scope

HOME-45 approves the internal, revision-bound control-design assessment and all
five categories, with combined automated/manual review subject to five
[incorporation conditions](../../compliance/soc2/HOME-45-decision.md).
The [scope](../../compliance/soc2/scope.md) and [packet v5](../../compliance/soc2/evidence/HOME-30-v5/packet.md) account for Fleet/Entra
and HOME-30 additions across the whole tree, named manual reviewers and open
findings. Prior packets and the original decision remain revision-bound history.

Scope approval is not control or risk acceptance. Ordinary main drift requires
evidence refresh; material boundary changes require a new decision. QA and
Auditor review remain separate. HOME-36 prepares a design evidence program;
operating collection requires a separate period/population/sampling decision
and execution authorization. No merge, deployment or stage promotion is granted.

V4 verifies every tracked source and embeds historical artifact/tree proofs so
a fresh squash clone can reproduce integrity checks without old PR commits.
Normal and optimized Python corruption fixtures are required. HOLD remains:
unsigned PR history needs an authorized signed-history plan, qualifying review,
effective-protection confirmation and fresh QA/Auditor acceptance. No bypass.

V5 pins the original unique v1–v3 revision/root/manifest bindings independently
of refreshed evidence. Regenerated and committed missing, empty, duplicate and
substituted history must fail in normal and optimized Python. HOME-46 selects
a signed replacement route but does not authorize preparation; HOME-47 retains
the operator/signing/protection prerequisite. QA and explicit Auditor disposition
of the P2 repair precede a concrete preparation decision.
